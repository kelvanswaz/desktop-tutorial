Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the Vintage Newspaper package?

The Vintage Newspaper package includes unlimited prints for 2 hours, full customization of your newspaper design, a high-quality vintage aesthetic, and digital copies of all your photos after the event.

Can I customize the photo booth backdrop?

Yes, both the Vintage Newspaper and Classic Photo Booth packages offer a custom backdrop as an available add-on. Please contact us for pricing details.

How long is the rental duration for each package?

Both the Vintage Newspaper and Classic Photo Booth packages include a 2-hour rental duration. Time extensions are available as an add-on for $80/hr.

How will I receive my digital photos?

For the Vintage Newspaper package, you will receive all digital copies after the event. For the Classic Photo Booth, digital sharing is instant via QR code, Airdrop, or email.

Is there an option for instant soft copy sharing?

Yes, for the Vintage Newspaper package, you can add instant soft copy sharing onsite for an additional $100. The Classic Photo Booth offers instant digital sharing by default.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much space do you need?

For the Vintage Newspaper setup, we recommend a space of at least 8x8 feet. For the Classic Photo Booth, about 6x6 feet is sufficient. This allows for comfortable movement and prop placement.

Can I customize the prints?

Absolutely! For the Newspaper Print Package, you can customize the newspaper header. For the Classic Photo Booth, you can add custom text or logos to your photo strips.

Is an attendant included?

Yes, all our packages include a friendly and professional attendant to ensure a smooth and fun experience for your guests throughout the event.

Do you provide props?

Yes, both packages come with a selection of themed props to enhance the fun and creativity of your photos.
