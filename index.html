How It Works

The Vintage Newspaper Experience

  1. 1. Strike a Pose: Guests enjoy the booth and take their photos.
  2. 2. Instant Vintage Prints: Photos are transformed into vintage newspaper style.
  3. 3. Take Home a Keepsake: Guests receive their unique printed newspaper.

The Classic Photo Booth Experience

  1. 1. Strike a Pose: Guests step in and snap their fun photos.
  2. 2. Instant Prints: High-quality photos print out in seconds.
  3. 3. Take Home the Memory: Guests grab their prints, often in protective sleeves.
  4. 4. Share Immediately: Options to share digital copies on the spot.
